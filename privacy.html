Tankful Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Tankful: Gas Mileage & MPG ("Tankful") is a fuel log for iPhone. We built it so that your data stays yours.

What we collect

Nothing. Tankful has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

Where your data lives

Your vehicles and fill-ups are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are synced through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), which only you can access. We cannot see it.

Notifications

If you turn on fill-up reminders, Tankful schedules notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Tankful Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Export and deletion

You can export all your data as a CSV file at any time in Settings. Deleting a vehicle or the app removes the data from your device; data in your iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
