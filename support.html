Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Open a request at github.com/hauhc93/tankful/issues (a free GitHub account is needed). We usually reply within a few days.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud. Tankful has no account and no server, so we cannot see your data.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account; your vehicles and fill-ups sync automatically. You can also export a CSV in Settings and import it on the new phone.

Why is my MPG missing after a fill-up?

MPG is measured from one full tank to the next. Mark the fill-up as "Filled to full tank", and if you skipped logging a fill-up, turn on "Missed previous fill-up".

How do I restore Tankful Pro?

Open Settings in Tankful and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tankful Pro.
